# Customer_behavior-Analysis

Customer Behavior Shopping Analysis

Project Overview -

The Customer Behavior Shopping Analysis project focuses on understanding customer purchasing patterns, shopping preferences, and buying behavior using data analytics.
The main objective of this project is to analyze customer shopping data and identify meaningful insights that can help businesses understand their customers, improve customer satisfaction, optimize marketing strategies, and make data-driven business decisions.

Project Objectives -

Analyze customer purchasing patterns and shopping behavior.
Understand customer preferences based on product categories, colors, sizes, and seasons.
Identify purchasing trends across different age groups and genders.
Analyze customer spending habits and purchase amounts.
Evaluate customer satisfaction through review ratings.
Understand the impact of discounts and subscription status on shopping behavior.
Identify customer purchase frequency and previous purchasing patterns.

 Key Analysis Areas -

Customer Demographics: Analyze shopping behavior across different age groups and genders.
Purchase Analysis: Understand purchasing amounts and popular product categories.
Seasonal Shopping Trends: Identify how customer purchases vary across different seasons.
Customer Satisfaction: Evaluate customer feedback using review ratings.
Payment Analysis: Understand customer preferences for different payment methods.
Discount Analysis: Examine customer shopping behavior based on discount usage.
Customer Loyalty: Analyze subscription status and previous purchase history.
Purchase Frequency: Understand how frequently customers make purchases.

Project Outcome

This project provides a better understanding of customer shopping behavior through data analysis. It helps identify important purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and business opportunities that can support improved marketing strategies and customer experiences.
